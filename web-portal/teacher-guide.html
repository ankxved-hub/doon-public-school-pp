Doon Public School, Etah — Teacher Login Guide

Illustrated Step-by-Step Guide for Android App & School Website

Official Website: https://doonpublicschooletah.in • Kasganj Road, Aruna Nagar, Etah

STEP 1 • ANDROID APP TEACHER SIGN IN
  1. Open the App: Tap the Doon Public School, Etah app icon on your Android phone. Confirm the bottom bar shows Cloudflare Edge Connected.
  2. Select Role = Teacher: Under STEP 1: SELECT YOUR ROLE, tap Teacher (highlighted in Navy & Gold).
  3. Enter Employee ID: Type the Teacher Employee ID issued by the Admin Office (for example: TCH101).
  4. Enter Password & Sign In: Type your password (tap the eye icon to double-check) and press Sign In to Doon Public School Portal.
Need an account first? Principal / Admin creates Teacher accounts inside Admin → Accounts and gives each teacher their Employee ID & Password.

STEP 2 • TEACHER PORTAL & MULTI-ROLE SWITCHING
  • Daily & Weekly DPT: Submit daily topics taught, homework assignments, attendance, marks, and parent circulars right from the bottom tabs.
  • Multi-Role Accounts: If Admin enabled extra access for you (such as Fee Manager or Principal / Admin), tap the Swap (⇔) button at the top-right to switch portals without logging out.
  • Live Cloud Sync: Entries saved by a teacher on the app immediately sync to Parents, Principal, and Website.

STEP 3 • WEBSITE LOGIN (https://doonpublicschooletah.in)
  1. Open https://doonpublicschooletah.in (or your Cloudflare Portal URL) on Chrome or any phone/laptop browser.
  2. Click the Teacher role button in the School Role Login card.
  3. Enter the exact same Employee ID (e.g., TCH101) and Password used in the mobile app and click Sign In.
One Login Everywhere: Teachers use the same ID & password on both the Android App and Web Portal.
